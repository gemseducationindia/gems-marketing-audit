Dates: leads, calls, visits and junk/lost count the leads created in the range (with everything done on them so far). Applications, enrollments, revenue and spend count what was paid in the range. Spend so far is Edelytics only (to its sheet's last day); other agencies show – until their sheets are added. CAC % of revenue: spend ÷ revenue for the same campus, channel and dates. Impressions, clicks and agency-reported leads come from the agency's sheet; CTR = clicks ÷ impressions. Compare agency-reported leads with the CRM's leads to see how many of their leads actually reached the CRM.

Leads: every CRM lead (duplicates and leads with no campus excluded). Channel: Meta = Meta Ads, Instagram Ads, Facebook; Google = Google Ads; Website = Website Enquiry; Others = walk-in, referral, open house, events and the rest.

Reached: at least one outgoing call. Dials: outgoing calls, not counting a counsellor's outcome note logged right after an Exotel call. Connect: an answered call that lasted more than 0 seconds. Connect rate: connects ÷ dials.

Visits scheduled / done: a campus visit booked / marked done in the CRM. Junk + lost: lead status Junk or Lost.

Applications: application fee paid. Enrollments: admission fee paid, or tuition/term fee paid where the admission fee was waived. Revenue: application + admission + tuition/term fees paid. These come from the admissions entry sheet and are matched to CRM leads by the parent's mobile number.

Paying students whose number matches no CRM lead are counted under Others, as no marketing channel can be credited for them.
