How these numbers are counted

Dates: CRM leads, calling, visits and junk/lost count the leads created in the range, with everything done on them so far. Applications, enrollments, revenue and spend count what was paid in the range.

Media comes live from each agency's sheet: Edelytics, EchoVME and Because. Reach is the agencies' daily reach added up, so over several days it counts some people more than once. EchoVME doesn't report clicks. Agency leads: the leads the agency reports from its campaigns; compare them with CRM leads to see how many reached the CRM. CAC % of revenue: spend ÷ revenue for the same rows and dates.

CRM leads: every CRM lead with a campus (duplicates left out). Channel: Meta = Meta Ads, Instagram Ads, Facebook; Google = Google Ads; Website = Website Enquiry; Others = walk-ins, events, newspaper, referrals and the rest. Open a channel to see its campaigns and then its ads (for Others: its sources, then campaigns). Spend shows against a campaign only where the agency's sheet names the campaign (Because).

Contacted: at least one outgoing call. Dials: outgoing calls, not counting a counsellor's outcome note logged right after an Exotel call. Connect: an answered call longer than 0 seconds. Connect rate: connects ÷ dials.

Visits scheduled / done: a campus visit booked / marked done in the CRM. Junk + lost: lead status Junk or Lost; reasons as recorded in the CRM (Invalid lead covers duplicates, wrong numbers and test entries).

Applications: application fee paid. Enrollments: admission fee paid, or tuition/term fee paid where the admission fee was waived. Revenue: all fees paid. These come from the admissions entry sheet and are matched to CRM leads by the parent's mobile number; paying students with no matching lead count under Others → No CRM lead.

Last 7 days

Leads created in the last few days have had less time to be called, visit or enroll, so their rates usually rise over the following week. Applications, enrollments, revenue and spend count what was paid in each week.
